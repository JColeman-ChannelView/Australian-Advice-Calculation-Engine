## Rounding / formula rules (primary quotes)

- **MCB** (U3, U10): "rounded down to the nearest 10 dollar multiple" / "rounded down to the nearest $10 multiple". Formula: "Concessional contributions cap × 100 ÷ charge percentage".
- **Minimum pension** (SISR Sch 7 cl 5, U24): "rounded to the nearest 10 whole dollars. If the amount ends in an exact 5 dollars, it is to be rounded up". Pro-rating (cl 3): by "the number of days in the financial year that include and follow the commencement day". Commencement on or after 1 June: no payment required (cl 4).
- **Division 296 proportion** (U13): "the following formulas are used (rounded to 2 decimal places)": [(TSB reference − LSBT) ÷ TSB reference] × 100, and likewise for the VLSBT.
- **Co-contribution** (U25 s10(1C)): "$500 reduced by 3.333 cents for each dollar" above the lower threshold. Minimum $20 (s11). The ATO says "Payment amounts are rounded up to the nearest multiple of 5 cents" (U18). Lower threshold indexation is rounded to whole dollars: under 50c rounds down, otherwise up (s10A(4)). Higher threshold = lower + $15,000.
- **LISTO** (U19): "multiplying your concessional super contributions ... by 0.15, rounding to the nearest cent"; minimum $10, maximum $500 (U25 s12E). From 2027-28 the maximum is set by an Act formula (U26 Sch 4).
- **CGT cap, ETP cap, untaxed plan cap, pre-2026 low rate cap**: "indexed ... AWOTE, in increments of $5,000 (rounded down)" (U2, U5, U8).
- **Concessional cap**: "increments of $2,500 in line with ... AWOTE" (U11).
- **Div 296 thresholds**: CPI, "increments of $150,000 for the LSBT and $500,000 for the VLSBT" (U7). The Act's indexation table specifies the same increments (U26).
